About Meeting Journey

Meeting Journey is an independent companion created by Chris Dolan to help people of all ages follow meetings in a way that suits them.

Choose a companion, move through the meeting, draw, write or just listen. Every activity is optional.

This is not an official app of Jehovah’s Witnesses and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Watch Tower. It links to official material in JW Library or on JW.org. It does not reproduce official articles, videos or artwork.

The app offers practical supports, not medical treatment. Families can choose what helps and skip the rest.
