Your drawings stay with you

Your notes, drawings, feelings, avatar choices and progress are saved on this device. They are not uploaded to us.

For parents and carers

There are no accounts, advertising trackers or automatic cloud backups in the app. Shared meeting outlines, links and app artwork download from our server. The hosting provider may keep ordinary connection logs; journal content is not sent.

Everyone using this app in the same browser or installed web app on this device shares its journal. There is no private account or separate profile.

Settings lets you export a backup file, preview and restore a backup, or explicitly delete personal data. A backup may contain private thoughts; choose where to keep it. Nothing is shared automatically.

Clearing website data or device storage management may erase the journal. Keep a backup. A different device starts fresh unless you import your file.

Official links open another app or website, whose privacy practices apply there. Your journal is not included in those links.
