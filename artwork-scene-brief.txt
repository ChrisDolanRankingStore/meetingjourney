MEETING JOURNEY: SIX WEEKLY WORLDS WITH FOUR SEASONS

Purpose
Create original painted background artwork for a calm, bilingual meeting
companion. A different world is selected each meeting week, cycling through
six worlds. Each world has winter, spring, summer and autumn artwork.
The app will select the season using the meeting date and a device-local
Northern/Southern hemisphere setting. Do not bake dates or hemisphere names
into the pictures. Weather is illustrated atmosphere, not live local weather.

Deliverables
Six worlds x four seasons x six images = 144 individual images.
For each world and season supply:
1. map: an elevated illustrated overview of the whole world.
2. gate: the welcoming entrance/start of the route.
3. river: a calm waterside listening location.
4. bridge: a pedestrian crossing.
5. village: a village path or small residential lane.
6. garden: a quiet garden/resting place at the end.
Home will reuse one of the five location images; no separate Home image needed.

Worlds
valley: river valley, rounded green hills, stone bridge, small village.
coast: sheltered coastal village, freshwater stream meeting the sea,
       footbridge, dunes, coastal garden. No dramatic storms or cliffs.
woodland: woodland settlement, forest stream, timber footbridge,
          cottage lane, peaceful clearing garden.
mountain: gentle mountain foothills, stream, stone footbridge,
          mountain village, sheltered garden. Route is safe and walkable.
countryside: rolling farmland, small stream, low bridge, country lane,
             orchard and flower garden.
lake: lakeside town, lake-fed stream, footbridge, residential promenade,
      quiet lakeside garden.
Make the geography, architecture, silhouettes and compositions genuinely
different. Do not produce six recolourings of the same valley.

Visual style
Use the supplied current app artwork as the reference for style and framing:
warm hand-painted watercolour/gouache, softly textured paper, natural detail,
welcoming light, readable silhouettes, gently muted colours.
Original artwork only. No copied illustrations or branded visual elements.
Not photorealistic, not flat vector art, not a videogame screenshot.
No people, avatars, text, labels, numbers, buttons, pins, treasure chests,
logos, religious symbols, borders, parchment panels or UI.
The app overlays its own companion, route controls, notes and treasure markers.
Keep busy high-contrast detail away from the central foreground and route.

Season continuity
First establish each world's summer geography and all six compositions.
Approve these before generating other seasons.
For each matching image, keep the camera, buildings, path, bridge, river,
garden and major landmarks in the same positions in every season.
Winter: bare deciduous trees, evergreens, light settled snow where appropriate,
        cool daylight, soft cloud or mist; path and bridge remain visible.
Spring: fresh leaves, blossom, small flowers, moist ground, gentle rain-cloud
        atmosphere or soft sunshine after rain.
Summer: full foliage, warm daylight, clear or lightly clouded sky.
Autumn: gold/rust deciduous foliage, fallen leaves, soft overcast light,
        occasional damp surfaces and gentle mist.
Adapt intensity to the world: coast has milder snow than mountain.
No heavy rain streaks over the entire image, blizzards, lightning, flooding,
dangerous weather, particles covering controls, or animation.
Season changes must affect the painted foliage, ground and sky, not just tint.

Map composition
Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio, elevated illustrative view, not a diagram.
Build a clearly visible, gently winding walking route connecting all five
locations within one coherent geography.
Use broadly consistent landmark positions across worlds so app overlays can
share their layout. Coordinates are percentages from top-left:
entrance around (24,86);
waterside around (48,71);
bridge around (51,53), with route extending towards (75,43);
village around (78,24), continuing towards (43,23);
quiet garden around (31,17), ending towards (14,11).
Keep these zones open and identifiable; do not paint markers at the coordinates.
Keep landmarks comfortably inside the image, with scenery beyond them.
Avoid giant central buildings, tiny unreadable paths, or important landmarks
right against the edge.

Location composition
Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio, human-height or gently elevated viewpoint.
Each location must recognisably belong to its matching map and season:
same architecture, vegetation, terrain, bridge material and water character.
Leave a calm central/lower foreground for a companion overlay.
Allow generous crop-safe surroundings for narrow phones and landscape tablets.
No essential object should be clipped by a centred crop.
Gate and bridge must be recognisable as those places; village and garden
must not be interchangeable generic backgrounds.

Output
Deliver separate PNG master files, ideally 2048 x 3072 pixels, sRGB.
If the engine cannot produce that resolution, use its native portrait 2:3
resolution and report the actual dimensions; do not upscale merely to meet it.
Do not deliver contact sheets as substitutes for individual images.
Do not bake compression artifacts into masters.
The app developer will create optimized WebP delivery sizes.

Naming and folders
<world>/<season>/<view>.png
World: valley, coast, woodland, mountain, countryside, lake
Season: winter, spring, summer, autumn
View: map, gate, river, bridge, village, garden
Example: woodland/autumn/bridge.png
All names lowercase ASCII. Keep geography consistent within each world.

Generation workflow
1. Create six summer maps, one per world, for composition/style approval.
2. Create each approved world's five summer location images using its map
   as a reference.
3. Create winter, spring and autumn versions using the corresponding summer
   image as the composition reference. Do not independently invent new layouts.
4. Compare each world as a six-image set, then each view across four seasons.
5. Deliver all 144 individual files; identify any missing or failed images.

Reusable single-image prompt
Create the [VIEW] background for Meeting Journey's [WORLD] world in [SEASON].
Use the supplied approved reference image(s) to preserve the geography,
architecture, landmark positions and camera composition.
Original warm hand-painted watercolour/gouache on subtly textured paper.
Portrait 2:3, gentle natural light, quiet welcoming atmosphere, crop-safe
surroundings and uncluttered foreground for app overlays.
Show [WORLD-SPECIFIC LANDMARKS] with [SEASONAL FOLIAGE, GROUND AND WEATHER].
No people, text, icons, markers, chests, logos, UI, frames or animation.
Return one individual image, not a collage.
