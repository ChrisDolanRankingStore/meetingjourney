# Companion artwork and prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Generated source sheets are saved in `artwork/companions-source/`; individual project portraits are in `src/MeetingJourney.Web/public/assets/characters/`. The source manifest records each sheet rectangle used for cropping. Existing original artwork was retained.

## Prepared-look prompt set

The following base prompt was used once per age range, with the age phrase substituted. The existing illustrated companions were style references. Adult source sheets were age and style references for the corresponding prepared sheets.

> A clean transparent production character atlas for Meeting Journey. Reference image is a style/age reference, not an exact edit. Four complete [AGE PHRASE]. Use a SIMPLER warm storybook illustration style, polished softly cel-shaded 3D-like volume with clean readable outlines and fewer fine textures, calm friendly faces, accurate age proportions, dressed modestly and smartly for a Christian meeting. Portrait canvas 2:3, exact TWO COLUMNS and TWO ROWS, one complete person per quadrant, generous EMPTY TRANSPARENT gap at BOTH centre dividing lines, all figures wholly inside their quadrant. No text, labels, scenery, halo or background glow. TOP LEFT: standing person with masculine presentation, East Asian features, navy jacket and trousers, fitted modest rectangular GLASSES drawn precisely on the eyes, leather MINISTRY SHOULDER BAG with realistic strap attached naturally to the shoulder and bag resting at the hip. TOP RIGHT: standing person with feminine presentation, Black features, natural textured hair, sage cardigan and modest long skirt, holding a TABLET naturally in both hands at waist height, no glasses. BOTTOM LEFT: person with masculine presentation, brown hair or age-appropriate silver hair, seated in a complete manual wheelchair, glasses accurately fitted to eyes and holding a PHONE naturally in one hand on lap. BOTTOM RIGHT: person with feminine presentation, South Asian features, age-appropriate dark or silver hair, seated in a complete manual wheelchair, burgundy cardigan and long skirt, leather MINISTRY BAG fixed naturally to side of the chair and a TABLET on lap. All glasses, bags and devices are part of each FINISHED drawing, sized proportionately and in correct perspective. Draw whole feet, wheels and heads with padding. Keep characters separated, centre horizontal division entirely empty, do not crop any body or device.

Age phrases: children aged 5–10; young people aged 11–12; young people aged 13–19; adults aged 20–34; adults aged 35–39; adults aged 40–59 with some grey hair and mature facial features; older adults aged 60+, silver hair and natural age lines, dignified and approachable.

## Original adult prompt set

Four additional sheets used the same 2-by-2 layout with standing figures above and wheelchair figures below, matching the warm polished 3D reference style. Adult age, modest meeting clothing, complete figures, transparent background and no glasses/bags/devices were specified. Details were:

- 20–34: standing East Asian man and Afro-Latina woman; seated European man and South Asian woman.
- 35–39: standing Black man and Latina woman; seated East Asian man and Black woman.
- 40–59: standing European man and Black woman; seated Latino man and East Asian woman, with mature features and some silver hair.
- 60+: standing East Asian man and European woman; seated Black man and South Asian woman, with silver hair, natural age lines and dignified expressions.

No further image generation was used after the request to conserve weekly credits; the generated sheets were reused for formatting, inspection and app integration.
