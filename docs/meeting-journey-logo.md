# Meeting Journey logo direction

The suggested mark is a quiet route with two waypoints and a small spark:

- The curved line represents the meeting journey and the freedom to move at your own pace.
- The two dots represent where the user is and where they are going next.
- The spark gives the mark a small discovery/adventure cue without making it noisy.

It is used as a compact rounded-square mark in the app header. The shape is drawn in CSS/SVG-style geometry so it stays crisp at phone, tablet and desktop sizes. The colour scheme changes the mark’s gradient while keeping the route and spark recognisable.
